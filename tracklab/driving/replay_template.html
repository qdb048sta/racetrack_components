What this demonstration does not claim

This is a conservative tracking run, not a minimum-time racing lap. It uses a new reduced Python vehicle, not the full native C++ simulator. The map, nominal grip, dimensions, and controller are educational. Sampled clearances are not a road-safety certificate.
